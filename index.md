# Privacy Policy for Jarvis

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Jarvis is a personal voice assistant built and used by Nathan for his own use. It is not offered to the public. This policy explains what happens to information when Jarvis connects to a Pinterest account.

## What Jarvis does with Pinterest

When Nathan asks it to, Jarvis uses the Pinterest API to create Pins and to read the names of his own boards so a Pin can be saved to the right one. Jarvis only acts on the single Pinterest account that Nathan connects, and only when he gives a command.

## Information Jarvis accesses

- A Pinterest access token, obtained when Nathan signs in through Pinterest's own authorization screen. Jarvis never sees or stores his Pinterest password.
- The names and IDs of his boards.
- The content of Pins he asks Jarvis to create: image, title, description and link.

Jarvis does not collect information about any other Pinterest user.

## How information is stored

The access token and any settings are stored on Nathan's own computer in Jarvis's local configuration files. They are not uploaded to a server run by Jarvis, because Jarvis does not run one.

## Sharing

Jarvis does not sell, rent or share information with anyone. Two outside services are involved in how it works:

- **Pinterest** receives the Pins Jarvis creates, under [Pinterest's privacy policy](https://policy.pinterest.com/en/privacy-policy).
- **Google (Gemini API)** is used to understand spoken and typed commands. The text of a command, including any Pin title or description Nathan asks Jarvis to write, may be sent to Google and is handled under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Retention and deletion

Information stays on Nathan's computer until he deletes it. He can disconnect Jarvis at any time by removing the access token from Jarvis's configuration and by revoking Jarvis's access to his account in his Pinterest settings.

## Children

Jarvis is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.
